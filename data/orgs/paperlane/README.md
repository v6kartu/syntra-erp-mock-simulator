# Paperlane (demo)

Stationery and paper converting. Fictional Picfare-like mill + converting loop.

- **Industry pack:** `PAPER`
- **Plants:** Pune mill (reels in kg), Kampala converting, Nairobi trade DC
- **Constraint story:** Creamwove 58gsm and woodfree 70gsm remaining kg vs school-term exercise-book explosion.
- **Customers:** Ministry of Education Stores, Savanna Trade, Lakeview Supermarkets
